# AIBRY Catalog Constitution

## Article 1 — The Music Vault Is Canonical

The Music Vault at `C:\AIBRY\music-vault` is the source of truth for the AIBRY music catalog.

## Article 2 — `project.md` Is the Front Door

Every song project must remain understandable by reading `instructions/catalog-structure.md` and then the song's `project.md`.

## Article 3 — Expand Rather Than Replace

Preserve meaningful history, revisions, alternates, prompts, decisions, and prior creative direction.

## Article 4 — Generated Data Is Disposable

Indexes, caches, reports, and derived views must be rebuildable from the Music Vault.

## Article 5 — Never Invent Missing Facts

Do not invent dates, credits, identifiers, links, ownership, release history, or approvals.

## Article 6 — Destructive Changes Require Approval

Moves, renames, replacements, and deletions require a reviewable proposal, backup plan, and explicit approval.

## Article 7 — Every Applied Change Must Be Reversible

Applied operations must be journaled with enough information to understand and reverse the change.

## Article 8 — Specialists Share One Contract

AI agents, humans, scripts, services, and hybrid workflows must all operate against the same canonical song project.

## Article 9 — Interpretation Is Not Canon

Creative Intelligence may identify patterns and propose relationships, but it must distinguish evidence, confidence, review state, and canon status.

## Article 10 — The Catalog Must Outlive Its Tools

The Music Vault must remain understandable and recoverable even if every agent, cache, index, service, and interface disappears.
